Privacy Policy – Moon Garden Calendar

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Moon Garden Calendar ("Moon Garden", "the app") is a lunar planting calendar for Android, published by Jati TSOOD. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. The public copy of this policy is at https://yaboai.github.io/VedicPanchangPrivacyPolicy/moon-garden.html.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't run any servers. We never receive your notes, photos, garden location or settings.
  • Everything the app calculates (moon positions, planting days, sowing seasons) is worked out on your phone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases of Moon Garden Pro are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Settings: your garden location (a city you choose, or one picked from your phone's time zone), calendar system, language, frost dates, reminder settings and your Pro status.
  • Garden diary: notes you write and photos you choose to add. Photos are copied into the app's own storage at a reduced size. The app only sees the photos you pick, never the rest of your gallery.
  • Reminders: garden tasks and reminder times. Notifications are scheduled by your phone; nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Home-screen widget: the next 15 days of planting information, saved so the widget can show them.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal permission. To find your city on first launch it reads only your phone's time-zone setting, on the device.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes all of this permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred),
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system and app version),
  • ad interactions (such as which ads were shown or tapped).

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Before any ad loads, the app shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform, an IAB TCF certified consent tool). You can accept or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, you may still see ads that are not based on your data. You can change your choice at any time in the app under More → Privacy and ads.

Everywhere. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Moon Garden Pro has no ads. Once you have Pro, the app stops loading ads altogether.

Purchases (Google Play)

Moon Garden Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and shares only the purchase status with the app, so it can unlock Pro. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Purchases are governed by Google Play's terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Adding days to your calendar

If you tap Add to calendar, the app opens Google Calendar with that day's details filled in, or lets you share an .ics calendar file with an app you choose. What happens next is handled by Google Calendar or the app you pick, under its own privacy policy. Moon Garden sends nothing until you tap.

Children

Moon Garden is a general-audience gardening app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on any server, there is nothing we hold that we could show you, correct or delete. Your diary and settings are under your control on your phone. For data collected by Google for advertising or purchases, you can use Google's tools at https://myaccount.google.com and the ad settings described above. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
